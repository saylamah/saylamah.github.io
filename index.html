Development approach

Scientific rigour connected to implementation

The objective is not merely to demonstrate a scientific or technological effect. It is to determine when, where and under which constraints that effect becomes useful, reproducible and transferable.

  1. Define Clarify the feed, products, operating regime, constraints and decision objective.
  2. Model Select the appropriate resolution—from transparent screening calculations to detailed kinetics and CFD.
  3. Validate Use experiments, literature, benchmarks, sensitivity analysis and quantitative acceptance criteria.
  4. Interpret Separate mechanism, transport, uncertainty, limitations and engineering relevance.
  5. Scale Translate evidence into pilot configuration, operability, safety, control and industrial decisions.
