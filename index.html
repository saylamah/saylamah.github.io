Development method

Evidence-gated path from scientific understanding to industrial implementation

Scientific and engineering activities are linked through one development logic rather than performed as isolated tasks.

1. Define

Clarify the system, objective, constraints, uncertainties and required decision.

2. Diagnose

Identify the governing chemistry, transport, thermodynamics and process interactions.

3. Model & Validate

Select the simplest defensible model—from screening calculations to detailed kinetics or CFD.

4. Pilot & Scale

Compare against measurements, balances, literature, benchmarks and predefined acceptance criteria.

5. Implement

Convert evidence into process requirements, pilot design, scale-up risk, operability and implementation decisions.
