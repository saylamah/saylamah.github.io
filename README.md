# Ahmad Saylam — Technical Website

Source repository for the professional technical website:

**[https://saylamah.github.io](https://saylamah.github.io)**

The website presents selected public and non-confidential work related to:

- reactive and thermochemical systems;
- chemical kinetics and reaction mechanisms;
- combustion, emissions, soot, and nanoparticle inception;
- reactor modelling and computational fluid dynamics;
- biomass, biochar, syngas, and waste-to-X processes;
- fuels, desulfurization, and process intensification;
- advanced oxidation and wastewater treatment;
- technology development, validation, pilot planning, and industrial scale-up.

## Website Role

This website is the scientific and engineering evidence layer connecting:

- professional expertise;
- publications;
- public technical repositories;
- reproducible methods and tools;
- selected technical case studies;
- engineering and research collaboration.

A separate consulting website is planned as the client-facing platform for services, engagement models, selected applications, and practical decision-support offerings.

The two websites will have different functions:

- **Technical website:** methods, evidence, publications, tools, and scientific credibility.
- **Consulting website:** client problems, services, deliverables, case studies, and collaboration models.

## Evidence and Provenance Policy

Public content is selected rather than bulk-published.

Historical programs, mechanisms, figures, datasets, and project materials are reviewed for:

- authorship and ownership;
- confidentiality;
- licensing and redistribution rights;
- technical integrity;
- validation status;
- suitability for public communication.

The website does not intentionally disclose confidential client or employer information, restricted operating data, proprietary project details, or third-party material without an appropriate right to use it.

Research hypotheses, screening frameworks, reconstructed models, and validated results are identified according to their actual evidence and maturity level.
