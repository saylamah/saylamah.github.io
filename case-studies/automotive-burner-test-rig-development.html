Automotive Burner Test-Rig Integration & Emissions-Oriented Development

Evidence-backed applied R&D work linking burner-system hardware, instrumentation, experimental execution, emissions evaluation, data interpretation and development-oriented feedback for an automotive exhaust-thermal-management application.

Engineering context

From test-rig readiness to evidence-based design feedback

The work concerned an automotive burner system used within an exhaust thermal-management development context. The engineering requirement was not only to operate the burner, but to establish a reliable experimental system with traceable fuel, ignition, air, measurement and control interfaces so that operating behaviour and emissions could be assessed under defined conditions.

System integration

Support for installation and commissioning activities connected the burner test setup with fuel, ignition and air subsystems.

Instrumentation

Pressure, temperature and mass-flow measurements were integrated with the experimental acquisition and control environment to support repeatable testing and interpretation.

Experimental execution

Planned tests were carried out across defined operating conditions, providing an evidence base for performance and emissions-oriented assessment.

Development feedback

Test results were analysed and translated into technical reports, improvement proposals and understanding of relevant burner and control-parameter effects.

Operating-window interpretation

Relating burner response and measured outputs to controlled operating conditions rather than treating individual test points in isolation.

Emissions-oriented development
