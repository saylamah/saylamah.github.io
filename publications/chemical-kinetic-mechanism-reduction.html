Reduction of large detailed chemical kinetic mechanisms for autoignition using joint analyses of reaction rates and sensitivities

Authors: A. Saylam, M. Ribaucour, W. J. Pitz, and R. Minetti

Document type: Published journal article

Journal: International Journal of Chemical Kinetics

Publication details: Volume 39, pages 181–196, 2007

Published online: 25 January 2007

Access note: This page provides bibliographic information and an independently written research summary. No publisher PDF or full-text version is hosted on this website.

DOI: 10.1002/kin.20232

Independent research summary

This study presents a reduction method for detailed autoignition mechanisms that combines reaction-rate analysis with sensitivity analysis to identify reactions that can be removed while retaining the predictive behavior of the parent mechanism.

The work examines how the threshold values used in both analyses affect the resulting mechanism size, computational efficiency, and predictive accuracy. It also establishes practical rules for selecting these thresholds and applies the method to detailed mechanisms for two reference hydrocarbons: n-heptane and isooctane.

The reduced mechanisms reproduce ignition-delay times and intermediate-species concentrations from the full mechanisms with good accuracy. The reported computational speedup factors are 5.9 for the n-heptane mechanism and 16.7 for the isooctane mechanism.
