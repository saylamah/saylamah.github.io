Development workflow

A structured path from technical question to implementation

1. Define

Establish the system, feed, products, constraints, uncertainties and decision objective.

2. Formulate

Identify the relevant chemistry, transport, thermodynamics and process interactions.

3. Model

Select a transparent screening model, detailed kinetics, reactor simulation or CFD according to need.

4. Validate

Compare with measurements, literature, benchmarks, balances and predefined acceptance criteria.

5. Translate

Connect the evidence to pilot configuration, scale-up, risk, operability and industrial decisions.

Private research-development programme

Kinetic Intelligence

Historical work on reaction rates, sensitivity, chemical reaction networks, mechanism reduction and dynamic adaptive chemistry is being reconstructed as a modern Python and Cantera framework.

The current development package contains tested mathematical and software foundations, but full Cantera combustion benchmarks remain a required validation gate before public release.

The framework remains a private research reconstruction. It is not yet presented as validated public software or an industrial predictive product.

Evidence, software and provenance policy

Public release follows technical and rights review

Archived materials are not uploaded automatically. Scientific value, software integrity and legal suitability are reviewed before public use.

Technical integrity

Equations, assumptions, units, balances, numerical methods and validation status are reviewed before results are described as reproducible or predictive.

Authorship and licensing

Historical codes, mechanisms, figures and documents are screened for authorship, ownership, licence conditions and third-party reuse restrictions.

Confidentiality

Employer, client and project information is excluded unless it is already public, non-confidential and appropriate for reuse.
