This portfolio presents selected scientific and technical work by Ahmad Saylam in applied physical chemistry, thermochemical processes, combustion, modeling, process development, scale-up, and sustainable industrial innovation.

The selected outputs demonstrate contributions across fundamental mechanisms, experimental and computational analysis, process optimization, and industrially relevant technology development.

  • Wastewater treatment, advanced oxidation, resource recovery, and circular industrial systems
  • Process intensification, desulfurization, cavitation, and sustainable energy systems
