How the evidence layer is organized

Knowledge objects and publication outputs are kept distinct

A publication is one representation of knowledge, not the knowledge object itself. The catalogue therefore keeps publication type, evidence status, identifiers and access routes distinct while connecting selected outputs to the Scientific & Engineering Knowledge Objects (SEKOs), repositories and case studies they support.

Peer-reviewed evidence

Journal and conference work supporting defined experimental, computational or theoretical claims.

Framework evidence

Open engineering methods and diagnostic structures linked to explicit decision questions and applicability boundaries.

Reproducible representations

Repositories, software archives and open outputs that make calculations, assumptions or reconstruction pathways inspectable.

Knowledge transfer

Professional articles communicate technical ideas to industry but are not treated as peer-reviewed scientific validation.

Combustion, Soot, Engines & Chemical Kinetics

Adaptive kinetic reduction · Open preprint

Causal Adaptive Reduction of Detailed Chemical Kinetic Mechanisms: Accuracy, Transfer to a Second Mechanism, and Runtime Limits

Author: Ahmad Saylam
Date: 15 August 2026
Platform: Zenodo

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21932685

Tests a causal adaptive mechanism-reduction controller with GRI-Mech 3.0 and USC Mech II. Evidence role: open preprint with a complete reproducibility supplement covering the frozen method, validation evidence, transfer cases, performance tests and runtime attribution.
