Benzene addition to a fuel-stoichiometric methane/O2/N2 flat flame and to n-heptane/air mixtures under rapid compression machine

Independent research summary

This study combines low-pressure flame measurements with rapid-compression-machine experiments to examine how benzene influences methane and n-heptane oxidation across high- and low-temperature regimes. A detailed kinetic mechanism integrating natural-gas, n-heptane, and benzene chemistry was evaluated against measured species profiles, oxidation products, cool-flame behaviour, and autoignition delay times. The comparisons show generally good predictive performance, while reaction-path and sensitivity analyses indicate that, under the investigated low-temperature conditions, benzene acts mainly as a diluent rather than as a strong chemical inhibitor of n-heptane reactivity.
