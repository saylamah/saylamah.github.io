Benzene addition to a fuel-stoichiometric methane/O2/N2 flat flame and to n-heptane/air mixtures under rapid compression machine

Technical summary

This article reports experimental and kinetic-modelling work on benzene addition to a fuel-stoichiometric methane/O2/N2 flat flame and to n-heptane/air mixtures under rapid-compression-machine conditions. The work contributes to the understanding of benzene interactions with low- and high-temperature oxidation chemistry, including ignition-delay behavior and reaction-pathway effects in hydrocarbon combustion systems.
