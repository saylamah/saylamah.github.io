Influence of lateral species diffusion and heat transfer on the evaluation of near-threshold sooting flames

Technical summary

This study investigates the influence of lateral species diffusion and heat transfer on the evaluation of near-threshold sooting premixed flames. The work combines numerical simulation and experimental interpretation to assess when burner geometry and shielding conditions may affect the apparent one-dimensionality of flat-flame systems. The results are relevant for soot-threshold analysis, flame-structure evaluation, and the design of reliable combustion experiments.
