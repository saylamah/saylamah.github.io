Independent research summary

This experimental study examines soot inception and early particle growth in laminar premixed ethylene-air flames operated near the lean sooting threshold at pressures from 1 to 10 bar. Laser extinction was applied to quantify soot volume fraction, while time-resolved laser-induced incandescence and transmission electron microscopy were used to evaluate particle size and morphology. The results show that soot loading and particle diameter increase with pressure and equivalence ratio, and that the sensitivity of particle formation to mixture strength becomes stronger as pressure rises. Agreement between optical and microscopy-based particle sizing supports the use of time-resolved laser-induced incandescence under the investigated near-threshold conditions.
