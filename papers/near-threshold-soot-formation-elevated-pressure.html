Near-threshold soot formation in premixed flames at elevated pressure

Technical summary

This paper investigates soot formation close to the sooting threshold in laminar premixed ethylene/air flames at elevated pressure. The study evaluates pressure-dependent soot behavior using optical diagnostics and particle-sampling methods, with attention to soot volume fraction, particle size, morphology, and near-threshold flame conditions. The work contributes to improved understanding of early soot-particle formation under controlled combustion conditions.
