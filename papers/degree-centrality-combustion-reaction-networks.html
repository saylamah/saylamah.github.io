Technical summary

This article applies combustion reaction network analysis to detailed chemical-kinetic systems. The work uses degree centrality to identify principal species during combustion processes and supports dynamic adaptive chemistry, directed-relation-graph analysis, and mechanism-reduction strategies. The approach provides a systematic way to distinguish influential species in complex reacting systems.
