Technical summary

This modelling study investigates the formation of reactive species from methane, ethane, and propane under HCCI-engine conditions. The work evaluates how fuel type and engine operating conditions influence the production of reactive intermediates, including oxygenated and hydrocarbon species. The study is relevant to low-temperature combustion chemistry, fuel reactivity control, and chemical-kinetic modelling of advanced engine processes.
