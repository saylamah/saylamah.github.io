Modeling study of reactive species formation from C1–C3 alkanes in an HCCI engine

Independent research summary

This study used single-zone HCCI simulations to investigate the selective formation of reactive intermediates during the lean oxidation of methane, ethane, and propane. Compression ratio, engine speed, and equivalence ratio were evaluated to identify operating windows that favour the production of oxygenated hydrocarbons, hydrogen peroxide, and ethylene. The results demonstrate that intermediate-species formation is strongly dependent on the selected operating conditions, particularly the compression ratio, and support the broader investigation of HCCI operation as a route for coupling fuel conversion with the production of useful chemical intermediates.
